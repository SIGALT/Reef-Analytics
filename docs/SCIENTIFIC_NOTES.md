# Scientific Development Notes

Reef Analytics is currently a synthetic morphometric sandbox for coral architecture.

Appropriate current uses:
- teaching fractal-like branching,
- metric sensitivity exploration,
- methodological prototyping,
- hypothesis generation,
- future comparison with real coral 3D models.

Not yet appropriate for:
- predictive bleaching-loss estimates,
- direct habitat-loss estimates,
- replacement of SfM/scanner measurements,
- conservation decisions without empirical validation.

Validation priorities include structured-light scans, photogrammetric meshes, repeated colony measurements, geometric standards, and sensitivity testing to resolution, point density, scale, orientation, and missing surfaces.
