# Contributing to Reef Analytics

Reef Analytics is currently an alpha research prototype.

Before contributing, open an issue describing:
- the problem or proposed capability,
- whether it affects geometry, metrics, ecology, UI, or exports,
- scientific assumptions,
- expected validation.

## Development principles

Contributions should:
- distinguish synthetic metrics from empirical measurements,
- avoid presenting uncalibrated scenarios as ecological predictions,
- preserve reproducibility,
- document equations and assumptions,
- keep mobile and desktop behavior consistent.

## Suggested branches

```text
feature/mesh-export
feature/df3d
feature/morphometrics
feature/real-mesh-import
feature/environmental-calibration
```
