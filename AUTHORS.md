# Authors and scientific contributions

Reef Analytics is a collaborative scientific software project developed at the intersection of coral ecology, three-dimensional reconstruction, morphometrics, spatial analysis, and scientific computing.

The original concept of Reef Analytics was jointly developed by Eduardo Juventino Ramírez Chávez, Sergio David Guendulain García, and Andrés Ramón López Pérez.

The project emerged from discussions about the biological and analytical tools required to quantify coral architecture and structural complexity. Andrés López Pérez and Sergio Guendulain García contributed the ecological, morphological, and 3D-analysis needs that motivated the platform, while Eduardo Ramírez Chávez translated these scientific requirements into computational workflows, analytical metrics, and the software architecture of Reef Analytics.

## Collaborative development model

Reef Analytics follows a collaborative development model in which biological questions and analytical needs are translated into computational methods, software tools, and quantitative metrics through continuous interaction among the authors.

Scientific priorities, metrics, validation procedures, and future development directions are therefore defined collaboratively.

## Authorship policy

Authorship and author order for specific publications derived from Reef Analytics may differ according to the contributions made to each study, dataset, software release, or manuscript.

Software authorship and citation metadata are maintained in `CITATION.cff`.
